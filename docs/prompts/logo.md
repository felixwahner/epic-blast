## Prompt used

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: main game title logo for the existing EPIC BLAST block puzzle game.
Primary request: Design one original, exceptionally polished, memorable "EPIC BLAST" game logo. A compact energetic wordmark with subtle exploding block-puzzle accents, suitable for a game splash screen and promotional artwork.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background, isolated logo, no background panel or scene.
Subject and composition: "EPIC" centered above the larger word "BLAST", tightly integrated into one strong silhouette. Oversized custom chunky italic arcade lettering, lively but beautifully legible at small sizes. Broad faces, precise dark navy outline, restrained beveled dimensional depth and crisp highlights. Two or three small square jewel blocks and a few angular pixel sparks radiate from behind the wordmark, making a controlled blast motif. Keep the lettering overwhelmingly dominant. Plenty of transparent margin around the complete silhouette. Wide compact composition, approximately 3:2 canvas.
Style/medium: premium contemporary casual puzzle-game branding with a subtle 16-bit arcade influence. Professional bespoke typography and clean production-ready edges. Expressive and playful rather than aggressive.
Color palette: EPIC uses luminous warm golden-yellow to orange, BLAST uses bright icy cyan to royal blue, with a deep midnight-purple/navy outline. Small block accents can echo coral and violet. Balanced vivid colors.
Text (verbatim): "EPIC BLAST". Only these two words. Spell EPIC E-P-I-C and BLAST B-L-A-S-T, in uppercase. Perfect spelling, no tagline.
Constraints: one finished logo only, original design, genuine alpha transparency, centered, fully inside frame. No mockup, no layout board, no extra text, no watermark, no imitation of another game's logo. Avoid excessive particles, washed-out glow, photorealism and clutter.
